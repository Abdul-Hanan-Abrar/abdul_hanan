## 19. Responsive design

The site must be fully responsive.

### Desktop

* Maximum content width approximately 1120px
* Large whitespace
* Two-column layouts where appropriate
* Featured project cards in two columns
* Voice cards in two columns
* Skills in four columns
* “What I Bring” in five columns
* How I Work in four columns

### Tablet

Reduce columns appropriately and preserve comfortable spacing.

### Mobile

* One-column layouts
* Hero image and content stacked
* Large buttons become full width where appropriate
* Navigation becomes hamburger menu
* Skills become one or two columns depending on screen width
* Project cards stack vertically
* Voice cards stack vertically
* Contact layout stacks vertically
* Resume buttons stack vertically
* Typography scales down smoothly without becoming cramped
* Maintain strong readability and touch-friendly spacing

## 20. Important design rules

Keep the website **professional first**.

Prioritize:

1. Clear communication
2. Strong readability
3. Credibility
4. Urdu + English identity
5. AI language-training positioning
6. Customer-support experience
7. Practical technical/project experience

Do not overcrowd the screen.

Do not invent additional jobs, companies, awards, certifications, statistics, testimonials, client logos, or achievements.

Do not change the provided wording unnecessarily.

Use subtle animations only:

* fade/slide on section reveal
* card lift on hover
* button transitions
* smooth navigation
* subtle audio-player motion

The final website should look like a **high-quality professional personal brand site for an AI language specialist and customer-support/operations professional**, not a template.

Make the visual hierarchy especially strong around:

**Abdul Hanan → AI Urdu Language Tutor → Customer Support & Operations → Computer Science → Projects → Voice Samples → Contact**

Also create clear reusable components/styles for:

* navbar
* buttons
* cards
* tags
* section headers
* timeline items
* audio cards
* project cards
* contact fields
* footer

Prepare the design so the **actual professional photo, resume PDF, project screenshots, voice files, Portfolio URL, and LinkedIn URL** can be inserted later without redesigning the layout.
